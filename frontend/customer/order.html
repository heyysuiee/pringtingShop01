        Before you place your order

        1. Add the celebrant’s name and any event date, time, and location that applies.
        2. Upload your design and reference files here, or choose one of the shop’s designs. No files need to be emailed.

        5. NO APPROVAL, NO PRINT. Review the draft in your portal before printing begins.
