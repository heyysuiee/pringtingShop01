How our ordering process works

From initial design to finished keepsake, we make custom printing seamless, transparent, and hassle-free.

01

Select Product

Choose from invitations, Sintra boards, mugs, stickers, or tarpaulins. Pick your desired dimensions, quantity, and finishes.

02

Submit Artwork

Upload your finished file (PDF, PNG, PSD, Canva link) or request custom design assistance from our creative layout team.

03

Digital Proof Approval

We perform pre-flight checks for bleed, clarity, and color balance, sending you a digital proof for 100% sign-off before printing.

04

Print & Delivery

Your order is printed on commercial-grade machinery, carefully packaged with protective corners, and shipped or made ready for pick-up.

GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about file preparation, production turnarounds, minimum orders, and shipping.

We accept PDF, PNG, JPG, TIFF, PSD (Photoshop), and Canva shared links. For the sharpest results, we recommend a resolution of 300 DPI for hand-held prints (invitations, mugs, stickers) and at least 150 DPI at actual size for large tarpaulins.

Our standard production turnaround is 2 to 4 business days upon artwork approval. If you are on a tight deadline, we offer an expedited Rush Service (24 to 48-hour dispatch) for an additional rush fee depending on the product volume.

No MOQ is required for Sintra boards, tarpaulins, canvas tote bags, and individual photo mugs — you can order as little as 1 piece! For invitations, party favors, and ref magnets, minimum orders start at 20 to 30 pieces to give you the most economical pricing per item.

Yes, absolutely! Our in-house layout artists can help turn your photos, themes, and wording into professional mockups. Simply share your event details, colors, and inspirations when placing your order, and we will send a layout draft for your review.

Yes. Our Sintra boards are made from solid high-density PVC topped with anti-fade laminates that won't warp or smudge when wiped with a damp cloth. Our tarpaulins are printed with heavy-duty eco-solvent inks engineered for outdoor rain and sun exposure.

We accept GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer (BDO, BPI), and cash on local store pick-up. For orders outside our local area, we ship securely via nationwide courier services with bubble wrap and sturdy box packaging to prevent bent corners.
