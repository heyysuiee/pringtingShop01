Services & products

Manage the whole catalog in one place: categories, products and their prices, order-form options, and the ready-made packages with their inclusions and additional-piece prices.

Live catalog: changes are saved to the database and appear on the customer order page immediately. Products stay on “Request a quote” until a price is entered. Existing orders keep the package details and prices they were placed with.

Product catalog

Select a product to edit its details, base price, packages, and order-form options (with their prices).

Packages

Ready-made packages across every product. Select a package to edit its name, description, inclusions, price, additional-piece price, and photos. Products without packages are ordered with the customize form only.

Service categories

Hidden categories and their products are not offered on the order form. Select a row to edit.
