Services & products

Manage the whole catalog in one place: categories, products and their prices, order-form options, the ready-made packages with their inclusions and additional-piece prices, and the shop-made designs customers choose from.

Live catalog: changes are saved to the database and appear on the customer order page immediately. Products stay on “Request a quote” until a price is entered. Existing orders keep the package details and prices they were placed with.

Product catalog

Select a product to edit its details, base price, packages, and order-form options (with their prices).

Packages

Ready-made packages across every product. Select a package to edit its name, description, inclusions, price, additional-piece price, and photos. Products without packages are ordered with the customize form only.

Shop designs

Designs customers choose with “Choose a Shop Design” on the order page; they only type their details (names, date, venue), never upload a file. Select a design to edit its name, description, service, products, personalization details, and image.

Standard size price list

Each standard size has its own price per piece. Products priced by “Standard size price list” (the Tarpaulin products) offer the active sizes of their category; the order total is the size price × quantity, calculated by the server. Customers can always choose Custom size, which you quote. Existing orders keep the size and price they were placed with.

Service categories

Hidden categories and their products are not offered on the order form. Select a row to edit.
